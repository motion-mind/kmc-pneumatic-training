How to use this site

Work through the lessons in order, or jump straight to a task. Every procedure is written to be followed at the box with gauges in hand. The Practice page has quizzes, troubleshooting scenarios, and a reset-setpoint calculator; the Reference page is a printable lookup with sources.

What you will be able to do

Read the air

Tell main air from branch pressure, explain what a restrictor and nozzle do, and know why clean, dry air is the whole ballgame.

Set up a thermostat

Verify direct/reverse action, calibrate at mid-scale, set day/night setback, and confirm the reset signal at the box.

Commission a VAV box

Choose the right reset type, set damper action, and calibrate minimum and maximum airflow on a CSC-3000 with a Magnehelic.

Troubleshoot by signal chain

Isolate air supply, thermostat, controller, actuator, and airflow sensor in the right order instead of swapping parts.

Quick pressure reference

Memorize this table before anything else. Nearly every diagnostic starts by confirming one of these values.

SignalTypical valueNotes
Building main air (control air)15–30 psiKMC CSC-3000 spec at port M. Often near 20 psi in the field.
Branch / proportional signal3–15 psiThe universal control signal from thermostats and controllers.
No air / loss of air0 psiDevices go to their spring-fail position.
Full command15 psi or moreFully one direction; main air is higher and must never reach a low-range gauge.
Velocity pressure (VAV inlet)well under 1 in w.c.Read on a Magnehelic with the box's own airflow chart.
Reset start point (CSC-3000)3, 8, or 10 psiFactory; field adjustable 0–10 psi.
Reset span (CSC-3000)5 psiFactory; leave it alone in almost every application.

Before you touch anything

Safety and scope
  • Pneumatic work usually sits beside line voltage, starters, and electric reheat. Lock out the equipment before opening a control panel or reaching into a box.
  • Control air is compressed. Release pressure before disconnecting tubing, and never point an open tube at your face or skin.
  • Never connect a low-range Magnehelic (for example 0–0.5 in w.c.) to main air at ~20 psi — it will destroy the gauge.
  • This site is training material. The device's own manual and the box's airflow chart always win over anything written here.
