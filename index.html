Pneumatic VAV Trainerdual duct · KMC controllers per deck

Click any tube to unplug it. Dual-duct boxes with two actuators use one KMC controller per deck (the thermostat signal is teed to both). Boxes with a single shaft driving both dampers 90° opposed have no controller — they run straight off the pneumatic thermostat. The hot deck is normally open, the cold deck normally closed, so the box always fails to heat.

Try this

  1. Unplug main air to one deck's controller — that deck fails to its spring (cold closes, hot opens).
  2. Unplug the thermostat line — both controllers lose reset and the box goes to heat.
  3. Switch to 1 shaft (opposed) — the KMC controllers disappear; the thermostat drives the linked dampers directly.
  4. Unplug a deck's sensor (H or L) — that controller can't measure flow and drives wide open.
