Try this

  1. Click the main-air drop to the controller (port M) — it loses air and the damper falls to its spring position.
  2. Unplug the reset line (port T) — the controller sees 0 psi and holds LO STAT.
  3. Unplug a sensor line (H or L) — the controller can’t measure flow, so it drives wide open.
  4. Switch to Reverse reset while the thermostat is direct-acting — the loop controls backwards and runs away.
