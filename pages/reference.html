Glossary

Branch pressure
The variable output of a pneumatic device — the control signal, typically 3–15 psi.
DA / RA
Direct acting (output rises with the sensed variable) and reverse acting (output falls with it).
Deadband
The neutral zone between heating and cooling setpoints on a dual thermostat where neither output calls.
ΔP
Differential pressure. At a VAV inlet it is the velocity pressure that indicates airflow.
EP transducer
Electric-to-pneumatic: converts a 0–10 V or 4–20 mA command into a 3–15 psi output.
FR tubing
Flame-retardant control tubing, specified as 1/4" (5 mm) O.D. for CSC-3000 fittings.
HI STAT
The high-limit setpoint dial on the CSC-3000 (right dial). Min or max depending on reset type.
LO STAT
The low-limit setpoint dial on the CSC-3000 (center dial). Min or max depending on reset type.
Main air
The steady supply that powers pneumatic devices, 15–30 psi at CSC-3000 port M.
Magnehelic
A differential pressure gauge used to read velocity pressure. Match the range to the box.
Master / sub-master
The room thermostat is the master; the CSC-3000 reset volume controller is the sub-master.
N.O. / N.C.
Normally open and normally closed — the position at 0 psi. Also a selectable damper action on the controller.
Nozzle-flapper
The bleed mechanism where a flapper partly covers a nozzle to set branch pressure.
P-E switch
Pneumatic-to-electric: closes electrical contacts at an adjustable pressure setpoint.
Reset span
The thermostat pressure change above the start point that moves the flow setpoint from one extreme to the other.
Reset start point
The thermostat pressure at which the controller begins resetting between LO STAT and HI STAT.
Restrictor
A tiny fixed orifice that limits supply airflow so the nozzle can modulate branch pressure.
Spring range
The branch pressure span over which an actuator strokes from one end to the other.
Throttling range
The temperature change required to move a thermostat's output through its full range.
