Unit 04

Setup & calibration procedures

This unit is the hands-on companion to Units 02 and 03. It walks the pre-checks, the thermostat calibration, and the exact KMC minimum and maximum airflow procedures for direct and reverse reset. Work the sequence in order; skipping the pre-checks is how a two-hour job becomes a two-visit job.

Before you open anything
  • Lock out and tag the AHU, any electric reheat, and the terminal fan before working in or around a box.
  • Release pressure before disconnecting tubing. Keep open tube ends away from your face and skin.
  • Use the correct gauge for the pressure you are reading. A low-range Magnehelic on main air will be destroyed.
  • Follow the manufacturer's manual and the box's airflow chart over anything on this site.

Pre-checks: do these before you calibrate

Every one of these has caused a "defective controller" that was not defective. Spend five minutes here.

  1. Air supply — Confirm 15–30 psi at port M and that it holds steady under load. Confirm the building dryer, filters, and main regulator are healthy. This is the number one root cause of pneumatic problems.
  2. Leaks — Leak-test the actuator diaphragm and fittings with a squeeze bulb. Even a small diaphragm leak keeps the actuator from stroking fully.
  3. Tubing — Inspect all 1/4" O.D. FR tubing. Trim stretched ends, replace tubing that is brittle, cracked, or discolored. Seat every tube snugly on its fitting.
  4. Airflow chart — Get the chart affixed to this box and record the design minimum and maximum CFM. Do not use a generic chart.
  5. Reset type — Determine primary air (cooling or heating) and thermostat action (DA/RA), then apply KMC's reset-type table. Write the answer down before you touch a knob.
  6. Damper action and stroke — Confirm N.O. or N.C. selection matches the requirement, and that the actuator drives the damper full open to full close without binding.
  7. Sensor — Confirm the high/total tap goes to H and the low/static tap goes to L, that the pickup is placed correctly, and that it is clean. If it is dirty, disconnect it from the controller before blowing it clean.
  8. Mounting plane — If the controller was removed, mount it in the final plane and calibrate there. Do not calibrate on a bench and then rotate it.
  9. Thermostat signal — Verify the thermostat actually varies pressure at T across roughly 3–15 psi as its setpoint is changed.

Procedure A — calibrate a pneumatic thermostat

  1. Confirm main air and let the sensor stabilize. A thermostat that just came off a cold truck will read wrong for a while.
  2. Measure true room temperature near the thermostat with an accurate thermometer.
  3. Connect a gauge to the branch output.
  4. Set the dial to the measured room temperature.
  5. Adjust the calibration screw until branch reads mid-scale, about 9 psi for a 3–15 signal.
  6. Verify action and range. Confirm the output moves the full range and in the correct direction for DA or RA.
  7. Adjust the setpoint if needed — the dial sets the target; calibration only centers the band.
  8. Record room temperature, dial, branch pressure, action.

Procedure B — CSC-3000 minimum and maximum, DIRECT reset

Use this when KMC's table says direct reset: cooling primary with a DA thermostat, or heating primary with an RA thermostat. Remember: LO STAT = minimum flow, HI STAT = maximum flow.

  1. Remove the thermostat signal

    On the CSC-3000, disconnect the tubing from port T and leave the port open. Temporarily plug the tubing (but do not plug the port). Alternatively, remove the plug from the controller's G port to relieve the reset pressure.

  2. Set the minimum airflow on LO STAT ΔP

    Adjust the center dial one adjustment at a time until the desired minimum airflow is read on the Magnehelic and is stable.

    • Turn the dial no more than about a half rotation at a time.
    • After each adjustment, wait about ten seconds and watch the damper shaft. If nothing moves, make the next adjustment.
    • If the box starts at a hard end of travel, turn the adjustment a full turn, then wait 20–30 seconds for the flow to change before judging.

    Zero minimum: do not crank LO STAT fully clockwise. Past zero it enters negative reset, which the controller must overcome before resetting, narrowing the effective span. To get a true zero minimum, adjust until the damper just cracks open, then back off about a quarter turn and verify zero airflow. KMC notes this is typically about two and a half knob rotations counterclockwise from fully clockwise.

  3. Restore the thermostat signal by reconnecting the tubing to T, or reinstalling the G port plug.
  4. Call for full flow at the thermostat — 15 psi or more at port T.
  5. Set the maximum airflow on HI STAT ΔP

    Adjust the right dial, a quarter to a half rotation at a time, until the desired maximum airflow is read on the Magnehelic and is stable.

    For better accuracy, relieve the pressure before each dial adjustment: pull the plug from port G, make the estimated adjustment, reinstall the plug, and let the damper stabilize before you read flow.

  6. Verify and fine-tune. Repeat steps 1 through 5 to confirm both settings are repeatable, then adjust as needed.

Procedure C — CSC-3000 minimum and maximum, REVERSE reset

Use this when KMC's table says reverse reset: cooling primary with an RA thermostat, or heating primary with a DA thermostat. Here LO STAT = maximum flow, HI STAT = minimum flow. Read that twice; it is the part people get wrong.

  1. Remove the thermostat signal

    Disconnect the tubing from port T and leave the port open, temporarily plugging the tubing (not the port). Or remove the G port plug.

  2. Set the maximum airflow on LO STAT ΔP — adjust the center dial, one adjustment at a time, until the desired maximum airflow is read and is stable. Same half-rotation and ten-second rules as before.
  3. Restore the thermostat signal by reconnecting T or reinstalling the G plug.
  4. Call for minimum flow at the thermostat — KMC's procedure has the thermostat put out 15 psi or more at port T for this step.
  5. Set the minimum airflow on HI STAT ΔP — adjust the right dial, a quarter to a half rotation at a time, until the desired minimum airflow is read and is stable. Relieve pressure at G before each adjustment for accuracy.

    Zero-minimum note: do not turn the HI STAT knob fully clockwise. If a zero minimum is required, adjust until the controller just begins to crack the damper open, then back off slightly and verify zero airflow.

  6. Verify and fine-tune. Repeat steps 1 through 5 and confirm the settings hold.
Direct vs reverse, the one-line version

Direct reset: LO = min, HI = max. Reverse reset: LO = max, HI = min. Everything else in the procedure is the same.

Verify the settings repeat

A calibration that only works once is not a calibration. Before you close up:

  • Drive the thermostat from minimum to maximum and back, and confirm the box hits the min and max you set each time.
  • Check that the damper is not binding anywhere in its stroke.
  • Confirm the flow reading returns to the same value, within tolerance, on repeat.
  • If you changed the reset start or span, re-verify min and max afterward — changing start or span can require re-tuning the end points.
  • Confirm any sequencing — reheat, a P-E switch — triggers on the reset range, not the actuator spring range.
